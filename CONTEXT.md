# POE inquiry language

POE preserves a person's inquiry while offering possible orientations. These terms describe inquiry, not proof or accepted meaning.

## Language

**Explicit human question**:
A question attributed to a human and retained in their exact words. Its presence does not establish its presuppositions as claims.
_Avoid_: Inferred question, hidden question

**Inferred question**:
A proposed interpretation of inquiry, attributed to its proposer and grounded in source material. It is not privileged access to the person's unstated intent.
_Avoid_: Actual question, accepted intent

**Claim**:
An assertion represented separately from inquiry. The existing inferred-claim label describes an inferred assertion, not an inferred question.
_Avoid_: Question, proof

**Proposed inquiry relationship**:
A source-grounded suggestion that one question may underlie another or may inform a claim. It is not established causation, answer fit, or human acceptance.
_Avoid_: Proven lineage, accepted relationship

**Human response**:
A human's recorded response to a question, which may correct, reject, or continue its interpretation. A response alone does not confirm truth, agreement, or closure.
_Avoid_: Validation, automatic acceptance

**Human correction**:
A human response explicitly recorded as correcting an interpretation while preserving the earlier question and proposal.
_Avoid_: Erasure, replacement truth
